SPECIFICATION NOTES FOR OVERSEAS BUYERS

What Changes When You Specify Japanese Materials

Japanese products are built to Japanese standards and Japanese dimensions. Most of the friction in a first order comes from four differences that nobody warns overseas specifiers about. Here they are up front.

ROLL WIDTH

Japanese Wallcovering Is 92cm Wide, Not 53cm

Sangetsu, Lilycolor, Sincol and Toli manufacture vinyl wallcovering at 92cm width in 50m rolls. The European standard is 53cm in 10m rolls. One Japanese roll therefore covers roughly the area of eight European rolls.

This changes two things on your side: your installer estimates fewer, heavier rolls and needs a wider paste table, and your per-square-metre maths does not transfer from a European quotation. We supply the converted figures with every quotation so nothing is estimated twice.

MarketWidthRoll length
Japan92 cm50 m
Europe53 cm10 m
North America68.6 cm8.2 m
EMISSION GRADE

Reading F★★★★ Against E1 And CARB Phase 2

Japanese boards, flooring, doors and adhesives carry a formaldehyde emission grade written as F followed by one to four stars. F★★★★ is the highest grade and the only one permitted for unrestricted interior use under the Japanese Building Standards Act.

F★★★★ is Japan’s highest formaldehyde emission grade. It may support an overseas indoor-air submittal, but equivalence and acceptance must be confirmed for the exact product and destination code. Applicable grade documentation is supplied per line item.

GradeEmissionInterior use
F★★★★LowestUnrestricted
F★★★LowArea limited
F★★ModerateArea limited
FIRE CLASSIFICATION

Japanese Fire Ratings And Their EN Equivalents

Japan classifies interior finishes under three levels defined by the Building Standards Act: Funen (non-combustible), Junfunen (quasi-non-combustible) and Nannen (flame-retardant), separated by how long the material must resist ignition — 20, 10 and 5 minutes respectively.

These are not identical to the European Euroclass system and cannot be mapped one to one. We provide the original Japanese test certificate where available so your fire consultant can assess the exact product directly rather than relying on a marketing equivalence.

Japanese classResistanceTypical use
Funen20 minEscape routes
Junfunen10 minCorridors
Nannen5 minGeneral rooms
MODULE & DIMENSION

The Japanese Module Behind Every Panel Size

Japanese interiors are dimensioned on the shakkanho module rather than on round metric numbers. The base unit is the ken, and panel goods are cut to it: standard sheet material arrives at 910 x 1820 mm, doors and flooring runs follow the same grid, and unit bathrooms are sold as fixed module sizes such as 1216 or 1620 rather than being built to a site dimension.

The practical consequence is that a Japanese unit bath or door set has to be designed into the opening, not fitted to an opening that already exists. Send us the rough opening dimensions with your enquiry and we will confirm which module lands cleanly before anything is quoted.

EXPORT BUYER FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask Before Sourcing From Japan

Clear answers on export availability, order volume, specifications, documentation and shipping.

Can you export Japanese building materials outside Japan?

Yes. Most Japanese building material manufacturers sell only on the domestic market and do not handle export. EST is a distributor and export partner for ten Japanese manufacturers, and ships worldwide direct from Japan under the original brand manifest.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Orders can be placed as FCL or LCL. Buyers ordering below a full load from any single brand can consolidate multiple Japanese manufacturers into one container, which brings mixed orders up to container economics.

Why is Japanese wallpaper 92cm wide instead of 53cm?

Japanese vinyl wallcoverings from Sangetsu, Lilycolor, Sincol and Toli are manufactured at 92cm width and typically supplied in 50m rolls, against the 53cm European standard. This changes roll yield and installer estimating, so conversion figures are supplied with every quotation.

What certification is supplied with the goods?

Documentation is confirmed per line item and may include a commercial invoice, packing list, Japanese Certificate of Origin, applicable JIS or fire documentation, formaldehyde emission grade and MSDS where applicable.

Which ports do you ship from?

Export desks operate at Yokohama, Nagoya and Osaka. Available shipping terms and destination costs are confirmed per enquiry.

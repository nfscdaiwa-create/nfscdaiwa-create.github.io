Product dimensions

Dimensions and packaging differ by model. Some Japanese wallcoverings use approximately 92 cm widths and 50 m rolls, but these are examples, not specifications for every product. Confirm usable width, repeat, waste and pack quantity.

Modules and installation

Verify opening dimensions, clearances, accessories and the local installation method for the selected product. Bathroom installation time depends on site preparation and work scope; no universal one-day installation is promised.

Fire and emissions documents

Japanese fire classifications and F★★★★ documentation are not automatic equivalents of European or other destination approvals. Obtain the exact product reports and have the destination requirements checked by a qualified local professional.

Electrical compatibility

Check voltage, frequency, power, grounding and the manufacturer’s export/use restrictions for each model. A transformer alone does not establish safety or suitability. Confirm documentation before purchase.

Size and packaging

E depend on model. Some Japanese wallcovering dey about 92 cm wide with 50 m rolls, but no be all products. Confirm usable width, pattern repeat, waste and pack quantity.

Modules and installation

Check opening size, clearances, accessories and local installation method. Bathroom work time depend on site preparation and work scope; no general promise say everything go finish for one day.

Fire and emission papers

Japanese fire classes and F★★★★ no automatically equal European or other approvals. Get the exact product reports and make qualified local professional check the destination rules.
